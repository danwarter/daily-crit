This week's crit · Lesson 35
The grey text is hard to read
Pale grey looks tidy on a big monitor, but on a phone in daylight, or for someone with low vision, it fades away. Normal-size text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.
Backup settings / Before
The problem
Light grey text at 2.3:1 contrast
The descriptions are pale grey on white, far below the 4.5:1 minimum for normal-size text. In bright light, or with low vision, the warning about data charges is easy to miss.
Backup settings / After
The fix
Darker grey at 5.2:1, still secondary
The descriptions now pass the 4.5:1 minimum, and they still read as secondary because they’re smaller and greyer than the labels. Check the ratio with a contrast tool, not by eye.
