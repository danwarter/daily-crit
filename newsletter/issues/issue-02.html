This week's crit · Lesson 21
An error stops people with no way forward
An error that only says something went wrong, then throws people out, ends the task for them. Say what you know, keep their work and give them a clear next step.
Bag / Before
The problem
1 vague error, 0 ways forward
“Something went wrong” doesn’t even say whether people were charged. OK is the only button, and it empties the bag, so people have to find every item again or give up.
Bag / After
The fix
2 ways forward, nothing lost
It confirms nothing was charged and keeps the bag. Error recovery is the way forward: Retry Payment and Use Another Card each say exactly where they lead, which is information scent.
