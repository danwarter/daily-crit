This week's crit · Lesson 28
The no button tries to guilt people
Guilt-trip wording on a decline button might win a few extra sign-ups, but it can make people feel manipulated. Make the case for yes, and let no be a plain no.
Shop / Before
The problem
7 words of guilt just to say no
To turn down the discount, people have to tap “I don’t like saving money.” That’s confirmshaming: it uses guilt to push people toward yes, and it may leave them annoyed at the brand.
Shop / After
The fix
Just “No Thanks”
“No Thanks” removes the shaming, so declining costs nothing. A fully honest choice also needs truthful offer terms and clear ways to accept or refuse.
